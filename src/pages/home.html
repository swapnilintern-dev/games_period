The Problem

Why Is School Sports Still Being Overlooked in India?

Ten gaps we see in school sports today.

  1. Free periods

    Sports periods are frequently treated as free periods.

  2. No structure

    Lack of structured weekly and monthly sports timetables.

  3. Few coaches

    Limited availability of specialised sports coaches.

  4. Neglected grounds

    Poor maintenance of playgrounds and sports infrastructure.

  5. Costly kit

    High procurement costs and poor-quality sports equipment.

  6. Little exposure

    Limited exposure to inter-school and competitive sports.

  7. Untracked progress

    Lack of student performance tracking.

  8. No pathway

    Absence of long-term development pathways for talented students.

  9. Disorganised events

    Poor organisation of sports events and competitions.

  10. Few activities

    Insufficient extracurricular opportunities.

Implementation

Our Five-Stage Roadmap

Schools can adopt stages according to their needs. Each stage builds on the one before.

  1. Sports Event Management & Equipment

    • Organise sports events and equipment management
    • Prepare an annual competition calendar
    • Introduce regular inter-house and inter-school competitions

  2. Infrastructure Development

    • Assess existing facilities and convert unused spaces into courts
    • Improve playgrounds and develop indoor facilities where feasible

  3. Professional Coaching & School Calendar

    • Introduce professional coaching and beginner/advanced programmes
    • Develop age-wise schedules and yoga and fitness activities

  4. Student Database & Performance Tracking

    • Create individual sports profiles and a centralised database
    • Maintain fitness records, track participation and record achievements
    • Generate student sports report cards

  5. Extracurricular Development

    • Cultural activities, photography and talent workshops
    • Science and innovation fairs and summer and winter camps

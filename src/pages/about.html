About Us

Built for Schools. Driven by Sport.

Games Period is an Indian sports development and school management company. We transform how schools approach sports, physical education, student fitness and extracurricular activities.

Our Vision

To make every Indian school a place where sports, fitness, discipline and talent development receive the importance they deserve.

Our Mission

To build an accessible, structured and sustainable school sports ecosystem across India by providing schools with the management, infrastructure, coaching, resources and technology required to develop every student.

Our Story

Sports Should Never Be a Free Period.

We started from a simple observation: most schools have the playground, the ball and the will. What they lack is a system that turns a weekly sports period into a programme that builds health, discipline and talent.

Games Period exists to be that system.

Our Approach
Plan, equip, coach, measure, then celebrate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do schools need to buy all the equipment upfront?

No. Schools can start with an assessment and phase in procurement against a budget plan.

Can parents see their child's report card?

Yes, subject to the permissions set by the school.

Can we adopt only one service?

Yes. Every service is modular, and you can start with the one that matters most to your school.
